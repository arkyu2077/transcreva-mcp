# Transcreva: Cloud-Native Portuguese (PT-BR & PT-PT) AI Audio & Video Transcription

Welcome to the official technical and developer documentation for **[Transcreva (https://transcreva.net/)](https://transcreva.net/)** — the cloud-native Brazilian and European Portuguese speech-to-text, Whisper Large-v3 + Pyannote speaker diarization, and automatic SRT/VTT subtitle synchronization platform.

## Official Web Pipelines & Endpoints

- **[Transcreva Main Studio (Transcrever Áudio e Vídeo em Texto)](https://transcreva.net/)** — Browser studio & REST/MCP workflow engine for MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, OPUS, MP4, MOV, and WebM recordings.
- **[WhatsApp Audio Transcription (.ogg / .opus to Text)](https://transcreva.net/transcrever-audio-whatsapp)** — Instant transcription for WhatsApp voice notes with punctuation restoration.
- **[MP3 & Lecture Recording to Text Converter](https://transcreva.net/converter-mp3-em-texto)** — High-speed Portuguese transcription for university lectures, podcasts, and voice memos.
- **[Academic & Legal Interview Transcription (ABNT / Speaker Diarization)](https://transcreva.net/transcricao-de-entrevistas)** — Multi-speaker separation and timestamped DOCX/TXT exports.
- **[Automatic Video Subtitle Generator (SRT & VTT)](https://transcreva.net/gerador-de-legendas-automatico)** — Word-level subtitle alignment for YouTube, Reels, TikTok, and courses.
