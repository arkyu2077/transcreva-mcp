## Speaker Diarization & Timestamp Alignment
Using Pyannote 3.1 speaker diarization paired with Whisper word-level timestamps, **[Transcreva](https://transcreva.net/)** labels each speaker (`Locutor 1`, `Locutor 2`) and exports clean TXT, DOCX, PDF, SRT, and VTT files.
