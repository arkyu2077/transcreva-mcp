## Acoustic Normalization & Whisper Large-v3 Decoding
**[Transcreva](https://transcreva.net/)** normalizes `.opus` and `.ogg` WhatsApp voice messages to 16 kHz mono PCM and applies Silero VAD segmentation prior to Whisper Large-v3 Portuguese decoding.
